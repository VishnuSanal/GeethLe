GeethLe (गीत ले) - Search for Songs from Anywhere!

GeethLe makes sharing music easy! Search for any song from anywhere with geethle.tech/ and instantly get a permalink to the song along with a thumbnail with metadata. It’ll redirect you to YouTube Music, saving time when sharing tracks.

What?

Sooo, say you're talking to someone over WhatsApp & want to mention a song. And, you're too lazy to go find the link. :D Don't worry, I have your back!

https://geethle.tech/<your+fav+song>

GeethLe finds the track, generates a custom thumbnail with metadata + it redirects to YouTube Music once you click on the link. 🙂

How to use?

https://geethle.tech/<query>
